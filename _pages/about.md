I am a PhD student at the [Centre for Digital Music](https://www.c4dm.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/), Queen Mary University of London, supervised by [Prof. Joshua Reiss](https://www.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/~josh/). My research focuses on controllable sound generation,with a specific focus on addressing the challenges related to simultaneously achieving high sound quality and model controllability. I develop methods that combine the direct control of procedural models with the sound quality of neural models, and controllable generative music mixing tools that let users shape the final mix. Previously, I received my Master’s degree in Scientific and Data Intensive Computing from University College London and my Bachelor’s degree in Material Physics from the University of Sheffield.
