I am a Ph.D. student at [Centre for Digital Music (C4DM)](https://www.c4dm.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/), Queen Mary University of London, supervised by Prof. [Joshua Reiss](http://www.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/~josh/). My research focuses on improving sound synthesis models design, with a specific focus on addressing the challenges related to simultaneously achieving high sound quality and model controllability. 
Previously, I received my Master's degree in *Scientific and Data Intensive Computing* from University College London and my Bachelor's degree in *Material Physics* from the University of Sheffield.
